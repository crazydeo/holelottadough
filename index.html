The starter behind the scandal

Meet Allie Kat.

It began with flour, water and the wildly optimistic belief that a jar on the counter could become a business. Allie Kat turned bubbly, opinionated and indispensable. Now every batch starts with her and ends with someone asking when the next drop is.

Hole Lotta Dough is a one-woman, one-oven operation. That means every bagel is rolled by hand, every loaf gets watched like a toddler near an open marker, and every order carries a little bit of home with it.
