Our story

Meet Allie Kat,
the real baker.

Every loaf, bagel and focaccia you order started with Allie Kat — our six-month-old sourdough starter who takes her job very seriously.

Hole Lotta Dough is a neighborhood micro-bakery run by Amber out of our home kitchen in Mission Viejo. No preservatives, no shortcuts, no industrial ovens. Just flour, water, salt, time and a lot of love.

Everything is made in small batches each week. When it's gone, it's gone — which is why you're here.
